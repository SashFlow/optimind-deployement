# Information Security Policy

**Owner:** Management  
**Review:** Annual or after material change  
**Applies to:** All employees, contractors, and systems supporting OptiMind

## Purpose

Protect confidentiality, integrity, and availability of Sashflow and customer data in line with SOC 2, ISO 27001, DPDP, and GDPR expectations.

## Principles

1. Least privilege and need-to-know access
2. Defense in depth (authn/z, network, application, monitoring)
3. Secure by design in product development
4. Privacy by design for end-user and KYC data
5. Continuous improvement via risk assessment and audits

## Responsibilities

- **Management:** Approve policy, allocate resources, management review
- **Engineering:** Implement controls, remediate vulnerabilities
- **All staff:** Complete security awareness; report incidents promptly
